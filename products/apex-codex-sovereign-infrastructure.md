# Apex Codex & Sovereign Infrastructure Suite

An automated, self-hosting executive command architecture and local decision engine for high-capacity operators.

The **Apex Codex & Sovereign Infrastructure Suite** is the command layer beneath consequential work. It gives founders, high-capacity leaders, and sovereign operators a private operating architecture for capturing decisions, transferring ownership, and converting finite attention into durable leverage—without SaaS sprawl or remote data dependencies.

This is not another dashboard asking for your attention. It is a disciplined local system: your records stay on your machine, your operating logic stays visible, and your next move stays unmistakable.

---

### What You Master Within

* **Local-First Decision Ledger & Cryptographic Verification:** Record the context, choice, rationale, and priority behind consequential decisions in a locally stored, hash-chained ledger. Verify the chain to detect changes to recorded events.
* **Asynchronous Delegation & Executive Capacity Engine:** Assign owners, due dates, and weighted priorities without adding another meeting. See open commitments and their relative capacity load in each weekly review.
* **Trauma-Sensitive & Neurodivergent Energy/Focus Monitoring:** Add optional, private capacity and focus check-ins to decision records. The system supports reflection without scoring, diagnosing, or blocking your work.
* **Automated Local Backups & One-Command Deployment:** Snapshot local records automatically after writes and deploy the lightweight Node engine with a single start command—no hosted service required.

---

### What’s Included

1. **Apex Local Command Engine:** A dependency-light Node.js CLI for decision capture, delegation, verification, and weekly summaries.
2. **Sovereign Configuration Blueprint:** Editable local storage, priority weighting, backup retention, and weekly summary settings.
3. **Executive Capacity & Review Cadence:** Commands and templates for recording ownership, reviewing open work, and producing a local weekly report.
4. **Deployment & Continuity Guide:** Setup instructions, local backup behavior, and guidance for scheduling recurring summaries on your own machine.

* **Format:** Local Node.js Software Infrastructure & Executive Command OS
